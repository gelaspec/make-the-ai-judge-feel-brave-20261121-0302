A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it. `main.py` writes it
to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, byte-identical on every run.

> At the edge of the water, one flat stone warms in the late sun. The tide comes in, covers it, and goes out again, and the stone is still there, a little cooler, a little darker, holding the afternoon in its shallow cup. Nothing is asked of it. The water will be back.

One image, no instructions: a stone the tide covers and uncovers, still
there, holding the afternoon. The courage is shown, not told.
